About Antriksh

Useful flight, built close to the ground.

Antriksh is a ground-control system for a modular UAV. We are making the distance between a good idea and a dependable mission smaller, one tested flight at a time.

FIELD NOTE / 01

The best airframe is the one a small team can understand, repair, and trust when conditions stop being ideal.

Why we started

One aircraft. More ways to help.

In delivery and disaster response, the last mile is often the hardest one. Roads disappear, information arrives late, and specialized hardware is expensive to keep ready. We are designing a modular platform that can carry a parcel today, share a location tomorrow, and keep improving between missions.

The console keeps the operator in the loop while giving autonomous systems room to grow. That balance is deliberate: useful now, more capable later.
